A few things to know.

What is a virtual profile?

A personal page that brings your introduction, photos and social links together in one place.

What does my pre-order include?

Your selected profile design and a one-year subscription. The pre-order offer is Rs. 6,000, including one year. The regular annual subscription price is Rs. 19,000. Contact us for setup details and availability.

Can I choose my own name and social links?

Yes. Share the name, introduction and social links you would like to include when arranging your profile.

How do I place a pre-order?

Choose Milo or Kumo, then enter your name, phone number and email. Select bank transfer to view the account details, transfer Rs. 6,000 and send your receipt on WhatsApp. You can also discuss your order on Telegram. Bank transfers are verified manually after you send your receipt.
